02

Connect the same addons

If your export includes addon URLs, they are kept automatically. If it only has addon IDs, convert once to see which URLs are missing.

Supply addon URLs manually

Use the original addon ID as the key. Separate addons need separate URLs. Do not substitute a different provider’s URL unless its catalog IDs match.

Your addons stay yours. Keep the same catalog addons installed in Fusion; this tool transfers the layout and its references.
